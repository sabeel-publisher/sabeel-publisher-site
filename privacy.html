Privacy Policy — Sabeel Publisher

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Sabeel Publisher ("the App") is the in-house publishing tool of the Sabeel Shorts

video channel. It lets the channel's authorized operator publish finished videos

to the channel's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

This policy explains what data the App handles, why, and your choices.

Who operates the App

Sabeel Shorts (Ottawa, Canada). Contact: sadenalnoor80@gmail.com.

Data we receive from TikTok

When the operator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the App, TikTok

provides us with:

  • An **OAuth access token** (valid 24 hours) and a **refresh token** (valid up to

    • 365 days), plus the account's **open ID**.

  • **Creator information** returned by TikTok's creator-info endpoint — the

    • account's nickname, avatar, and which privacy and interaction settings the

    account is allowed to use — shown to the operator on the posting screen before

    anything is published.

    We do not receive the operator's TikTok password. We do not request scopes beyond

    `video.publish` and `video.upload`.

    Data we handle about content

  • The **video files and captions** the operator chooses to publish. Files pass

    • through the App's server only to be uploaded to TikTok and are not kept after

    the upload completes.

  • A **publishing record** for each post: date, the TikTok post URL/ID returned by

    • TikTok, and its status. This is the channel's own production log.

    How data is used

    Only to operate the publishing flow the operator asked for: authenticate the

    account, display the creator info TikTok requires to be shown before posting,

    upload the selected video with the settings the operator chose, and confirm the

    post is live. We do **not** sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with

    third parties other than TikTok itself, as required for the upload.

    Storage and security

    Tokens are stored server-side, access-restricted, and never embedded in web

    pages, client code, or logs. Publishing records are kept in the channel's

    production registry for as long as the channel operates.

    Retention and deletion

    The operator can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings

    (Settings → Security → Apps and websites), which invalidates the tokens. To have

    stored tokens and publishing records deleted, email sadenalnoor80@gmail.com and

    we will delete them within 30 days, except records we must keep for the channel's

    own accounting of published content (post URLs of already-public videos).

    Children

    The App is an operator tool for an adult-run channel and is not directed at

    children.

    Changes

    Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
