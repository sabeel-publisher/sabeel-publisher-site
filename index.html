Sabeel Publisher

Sabeel Publisher is the in-house publishing tool of Sabeel Shorts, a short-form educational video channel producing Arabic Islamic-history and dawah content with English subtitles.

The channel's authorized operator uses this tool to publish finished, fully-reviewed videos to the channel's own TikTok account (@sadenalnoor312) through TikTok's official Content Posting API. The operator signs in with TikTok, sees the creator info for the account, previews the exact video and caption, sets the commercial-content disclosures, and confirms before anything is published.
